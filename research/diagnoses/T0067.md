## Signal (what T0022 left)
T0022's exact in-house kernel (D0003) was accurate but could not run frozen E001. B1/B2 level sequences were monotone
but not steady, so the frozen rule refused them; T0022 had approximated annular sectors by squares, with surface
cells of 1.4 δ → 0.65 δ. C1 level 1 took 21 min, about 20 M wire-to-plane pairs evaluated by quadrature. Layout
solves took over 30 min.

Classification: **implementation** (T0022's discretization and solver strategy), not model or experimental. Confidence
0.8 for the wire cases (measured end to end); 0.6 for the full E001 projection (bars and B3/B4 were not prototyped).

## What was built (prototype, `l2proto.py`)
- **Wire.** Each straight edge has n_e = C_k equal segments, with C_k = 9, 12, 16, 21, 28 at levels 1–5 (realized
  ratios 1.333 / 1.333 / 1.3125 / 1.333). The cross-section has C_k annuli, graded so that the surface cell is
  δ(f_max)/2 at level 1, with harmonics cos/sin(mφ) for m ≤ P. The current density is 1_a(ρ) T_m(φ)/A_a.
  - **Coaxial couplings** (same edge, all segment offsets, Toeplitz): the log part is exact, from the expansion
    ln|x−x'| = ln ρ> − Σ (1/m)(ρ</ρ>)^m cos mθ, integrated in closed form over annulus pairs. The smooth remainder of
    the axially integrated Neumann kernel uses Gauss × trapezoid (spectral). The |d| kink of abutting segments uses
    graded Gauss with triangle splitting. These couplings are diagonal in (m, cos/sin) by rotational symmetry.
  - **Other parallel edges and the plane** couple through moment-matched proxy filaments. They are exact for
    transverse Taylor terms up to degree P, with truncation error ~ (r/D)^(P+1). Perpendicular pairs are exactly 0.
  - Harmonics m > P couple only coaxially and carry no source, so they are exactly zero and are not solved for.
    *Disclosure:* the reported `fil_w` = πr/C_k therefore refines only nominally beyond P. The measured effect of
    P → P+2 is ≤ 8e-15 (below).
- **Plane.** Uniform node grid with n_x = 4 C_k (C1: 36 … 112) and graded layers (top layer δ/2 at level 1, growth ×2).
  - The plane–plane kernel is computed once per distinct (Δi, Δj, layer pair): Hoer–Love (the frozen runner's `_hl_f`)
    for |Δ| ≤ 2 cells, and Gauss-filament quadrature beyond. It is applied by FFT (block-Toeplitz).
  - *Disclosure:* the deep layers do not refine; their count grows by about 1 per level. `plane_layer` reports the top layer.
- **Solve.** Saddle system [M, −C; Cᵀ, 0] with M = R + jωL_p. Dense LU when there is no plane and n ≤ 16 k; otherwise
  GMRES with a block preconditioner (per-edge wire blocks, per-branch layer blocks, sparse Schur on the nodes).
- **Rigorous solve bound.** Z = xᵀMx, stationary, after projecting x exactly onto KCL. Then |δZ| ≤ ‖P(Mx)‖²/r_min,
  because Re M = R is diagonal ≥ r_min, so ‖K⁻¹‖ ≤ 1/r_min on the KCL null space. A rounding term ε·n·|x|ᵀ|M||x| is
  added. This bound is reported as `solver_tol`.
  - It makes an *iterative* solve compliant with the frozen budget. That answers T0022 attempt 2's objection that an
    iterative solve could not be bounded.

## Q3 — full E001 projection (all 10 cases, levels 1–4/5, 3–4 frequencies, scaling solves) [I]
- **Measured serial cost on this Mac, levels 1–4:**
  - B1: 1 s.
  - B2: 12 min with dense LU; about 4 min projected with GMRES.
  - C0: 9 min.
  - C1: 11 min for levels 1–3, plus level 4, which did not finish within 21.5 min on this Mac [F]; projected 25–40 min with chunked kernels [I].
- **Not prototyped:**
  - B3 and B4: about 15–25 min each with GMRES.
  - Bars A1–A4: T0022 attempt 4 measured A1 level 4 in 4 s. Toeplitz reuse in A3/A4 gives ≤ 5 min each [S].
  - Scaling solves (A1, B2, C1 at the final level, one frequency, original + scaled): about 10–15 min, dominated by C1.
  - Analytic phase and rule self-check: not timed here; assume ≤ 10 min [S].
- **Total, Mac serial:** about 120–180 min, inside `timeout_min` = 240 but with a margin of only 1.3–2×. The Mac is therefore marginal; the VM is the recommended host.
  - Running cases in parallel processes is limited by the 17 GB memory, mainly C1 level 4.
  - Expected wall ≈ 60–90 min.
- **GCP 32–88 vCPU, ≥ 256 GB:** every case in its own process, so wall ≈ the C1 chain plus its scaling solve.
  - That is about 45–75 min, at an assumed per-core speed 0.6–1× this Mac.
  - [I] The large VM buys parallelism and memory headroom, not the C1 chain itself, which is serial in levels.
  - Parallelizing the wire–plane kernel over cores would roughly halve it.
- `vm_bench.py` (full mode: B1 levels 1–5; B2, C0, C1 levels 1–4) measures exactly these numbers on the VM.

## Q4 — E002 scale (per generated half-bridge layout) [I]
- **Inventory [F]** (`generate_layouts(1, seed=7, ...)`):
  - 2 dies/switch: 12 wires (72 inclined straight pieces, 65 mm).
  - 6 dies/switch: 36 wires (216 pieces, 196 mm).
  - Both: top Cu traces 1 825 mm² (66 × 34 mm bounding box, 0.3 mm thick, 0.38 mm above the backside Cu), backside
    Cu 68 × 38 × 0.3 mm, baseplate 86 × 56 × 3 mm, 4 die shorts.
- **Discretization E002 needs:** a reference error well below the 12 % MAPE gate, so about 1 %.
  - The level-1 settings here already give 1e-4 for wires and 1.3e-3 for C1.
  - Lateral cells ≲ the 0.38 mm ceramic gap, about 0.35 mm, with about 6 graded layers.
  - Wires need m ≤ 2 (bond-wire pitch ≥ 0.6 mm).
- **Unknowns:**
  - Traces: about 15 k nodes, so about 180 k unknowns.
  - Backside Cu: about 250 k.
  - Baseplate at 1 mm: about 60 k.
  - Wires: about 30 k (2 dies/switch) or about 90 k (6).
  - Total about 0.5–0.6 M at one frequency (20 MHz).
- **Cost:** all three Cu layers can share one lateral grid (masked for the traces), so every Cu–Cu block is Toeplitz
  and FFT-applied.
  - At the measured C1 rate (about 0.85 s per GMRES iteration at 78 k, close to linear in n) and 100–200 iterations:
    about 10–25 min per layout per process.
  - On a 256 GB VM, at 10–20 GB per process: 12–20 concurrent, so about 30–100 layouts per hour.
  - 6 dies/switch is about 1.2× the 2 dies/switch cost; the planes dominate.
  - Uncertainty ×3: the iteration count with galvanic trace ports and die shorts is unmeasured.
- **Not yet built:**
  - a non-parallel (inclined) wire kernel (closed-form non-parallel filament mutual [Grover] through the same proxies);
  - masked trace grids with ports and shorts;
  - two-plane stacks.
- These are about 2–3 weeks of focused engineering [S]. T0024 should treat this throughput as a design estimate to be
  re-measured once the layout path exists.

## Q5 — verdict
- **Go for E001 as registered** (no re-registration).
  - Every rigor element held on the cases measured: frozen cases, 2 % criterion, refinement rule with ratio check ≤ 1,
    and solver budget with a rigorous bound.
  - B1, B2 and C0 converge at level 4.
  - C1 errors are 1e-3 to 3e-4 with a first-order steady trend.
- **Cost of the E001 L2 build** [S]: 6–9 working days. That covers productionizing `l2proto` into `src/moduleforge/solvers/l2.py`:
  - bars with the D0003 Hoer–Love kernel and Toeplitz reuse;
  - B3/B4 two-port and ties;
  - a geometry-derived `mesh_hash`;
  - stated kernel-error bounds folded into `solver_tol`;
  - chunked and parallel wire–plane kernels;
  - unit tests (analytic limits, P and quadrature convergence, reciprocity, scaling).
- **E002:** go, at the throughput estimate above. Its layout path is a separate 2–3 week build.
