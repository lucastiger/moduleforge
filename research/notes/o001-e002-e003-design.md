## 0. Decisions for the founder at registration
1. **Sanity-check name `l2_converged` is kept, with a new meaning.** The packet says "replace `l2_converged` with
   `l2_extrapolation_verified`", but its definition-of-done smoke assertion requires a check named `l2_converged`.
   I satisfied both: `l2_extrapolation_verified` is exactly the founder's phase-1 gate (§3.2). `l2_converged` now
   applies conv_tol = 1 % to the *predicted reference error of every layout* (the first sentence of packet item (2)).
   Rename it before registration if you want the literal "replace". Renaming changes the runner hash.
2. **The gate counts solver_tol(k*+1) in the contraction band, not in the agreement test.** This is what the packet
   specifies. A gate that also moved the three-level limit by its stated solver bound would fail **all five** dev layouts
   (worst case 1.09-1.98 %, §2.3). Please confirm the point-agreement reading.
3. **The reference band is wide**, because the stated k*+1 solver bound dominates it (dev: per-layout band from
   −2.9 %/+0.7 % up to −7.9 %/+1.9 % around L2_inf). The primary metrics read the point L2_inf. The `*_refband_*`
   readings are conservative (§3.3).
4. **timeout_min = 1560 (26 h).** The nominal wall time is 19.4 h, inside the 24 h requirement. The kill limit sits above
   24 h so that a uniformly 25 % slower VM (24.2 h) does not destroy a ~172 CPU-h run. Use 1440 if 24 h must be a hard
   kill limit. That leaves a 24 % margin. To finish by 2026-10-20 even at the kill limit, start by 2026-10-18 ~22:00.
5. **2-die check stratum: side_by_side:2.** Reason (decided a priori, not from any pass/fail): it is the only family ×
   die-count-extreme cell with no refinement data at all. The checked set then covers every family at 6 dies plus the
   unmeasured 2-die cell.
6. Add E002 to `compute.burst_experiments`.

## 1. Pre-flight evidence (facts; dev seeds; `research/l2_layout_vm/cost.json`, `research/l2_layout_vm2/cost.json`)
Steps are normalised by L(k*): d1 = (L(k*) − L(k*−1))/L(k*) and d2 = (L(k*+1) − L(k*))/L(k*).
r = d2/d1 = (L(k*+1) − L(k*))/(L(k*) − L(k*−1)). L3 = L(k*) + (L(k*+1) − L(k*))/(1 − r), the three-level (Aitken)
limit. t(k*+1) = solver_tol_rel_L at 0.125 mm.

| side_by_side:6 (s3) | −1.153 % | −0.702 % | 0.6089 | +1.83 % | 0.87 % | 1.6e-3 | [0.460, 0.761] |
| interleaved:2 (s7) | −0.778 % | −0.484 % | 0.6218 | +1.30 % | 0.62 % | 1.4e-3 | [0.442, 0.803] |

### 1.1 What the solver bound does to the reference (inference)
- **Effect on L3.** ∂L3/∂L(k*+1) = 1/(1 − r)² ≈ 5.5-6.1. A t(k*+1) of 1.1-1.6e-3 therefore moves L3 by 0.84-1.81 %.
- **Effect on r.** It widens each r interval to about ±0.06 (6-die) or ±0.18 (interleaved:2, small d1).
- **Effect on the band.** Hull over the 5 dev layouts is [0.442, 0.803]. For the dev check structure it is the same.
- **Is the bound realised? (Inference.)** Suppose each L(k*+1) error were independent and uniform at the full bound. Then
  r would scatter by SD t/(|d1|√3) ≈ 0.035-0.10 per layout. The observed deviations from the mean r give Σ(dev/σ)² = 0.65
  on 4 dof, P(χ²₄ ≤ 0.65) = 0.043. So the real errors are probably well below the bound, or strongly correlated across
  layouts.
- *Speculation:* r falls as |d1| grows (0.622 at |d1| = 0.78 % → 0.573 at 2.11 %). A common relative error of about
  −6e-4 in L(k*+1) would produce that trend, and it lies inside the stated bound. A common relative error shifts every
  L2_inf by a similar fraction, which leaves Spearman unchanged.

## 2. Why the extrapolated reference (rationale)
- **Facts.** The T0076 gate measured a *step*, not an *error*. On 6-die layouts the step exceeds 1 % while the error of
  L2(k*) is 2-3 %. A 0.0625 mm level (k*+2) is unaffordable: the 0.125 mm level is already 8-9 CPU-h and 35-39 GB.
- **Inference.** Richardson extrapolation with a ratio measured on checked layouts uses information the run already
  has: every layout costs only k*−1 extra (0.2-0.4 CPU-h). It turns a 1.3-2.9 % bias into a 0.05-0.39 % cross-validated
  error on dev data, which is a 5-10× better reference for under 30 % more cost.
- **Method sources.**
  - The geometric-ladder model L_k = L∞(1 + c q^k) gives L∞ − L_k = D_k q/(1 − q). That is Richardson extrapolation at
    constant refinement ratio, equivalent to Aitken Δ² (derived; the reviewers T0094-T0097 verified β = −d2/(1 − r)).
  - Roache's safety factor 1.25 for ≥ 3-grid studies comes from P. J. Roache, J. Fluids Eng. 116(3):405-413 (1994),
    cited as [ROA] in `experiments/runners/e001_reference.py`.
- **What would show the method wrong.**
  - A checked layout that does not contract.
  - A leave-one-out extrapolation more than 1 % from the layout's own three-level limit.
  - A contraction outside the other layouts' band. This is the phase-1 gate.
  - A sample layout whose first step is so large that the observed contraction spread implies ≥ 1 % reference error
    (`l2_converged`).
  - On the evidence side: `l2_extrap_loo_err_max` near 1 %, `l2_d1_ratio_max` ≫ 1, or `spearman_L2inf_vs_L2kstar`
    well below 1.

## 3. E002 design
### 3.1 Sample, levels, phases
- **Sample.** `generate_layouts(90, sealed seed)`, 6 layouts per (family × dies 2-6) stratum (`sample_diverse`). Top
  decile k = 9 and top quintile w = 18, both exact. No exclusions: any failure invalidates the run.
- **L1.** Default mesh at 5/20/80 MHz, and hmax 2/1/0.5 mm at 20 MHz (F3 only).
- **L2.** `solvers.l2_layout` at 20 MHz. **Every layout** is solved at k*−1 (0.5 mm) and k* (0.25 mm). The **m = 4
  checked layouts** are also solved at k*+1 (0.125 mm): the first layout of u_shaped:6, interleaved:6, side_by_side:6 and
  side_by_side:2, by generation order. The runner refuses a config that drops a required stratum.
- **Phase 1:** the checked layouts at all three levels, plus C1 via `l2` and via the `l2_layout` internals. Then the
  gate: `l2_extrapolation_verified`, `l2_converged` on the checked layouts, both C1 checks, and solver health and
  provenance of these solves. **If the gate fails, no other solve starts.** Every check is still reported, no metric is
  computed, and the runner exits 0.
- **Phase 2:** k* and k*−1 of the other 86 layouts.
- **Order before any solve:** config/spec checks, DRC + lattice at all three levels, then a dry pass of the whole
  analysis on placeholder ladders. The dry pass covers the valid, aborted and failed-solve paths.

### 3.2 Reference and checks
**Reference definitions.**
- **Point reference:** L2_inf,i = L_i(k*) + D1_i·ŝ, with D1 = L(k*) − L(k*−1), ŝ = r̂/(1 − r̂), r̂ = mean r of the 4
  checked layouts.
- **Uncertainty (common-mode):** L(k*) + s·D1 for s ∈ [s_lo, s_hi], where s_lo = (2 − FS)·r_lo/(1 − r_lo),
  s_hi = FS·r_hi/(1 − r_hi), FS = 1.25.
  - [r_lo, r_hi] is the hull of the checked layouts' r over the 8 corners of each level's L·(1 ± solver_tol_rel_L).
  - FS enters as in E001, which multiplies its Richardson tail by FS. Here the extrapolated correction is trusted to
    ±(FS − 1) = ±25 % of itself. The FS > 1 end is E001's convention. The 2 − FS end is my symmetric choice, stated here.
- **Never a dev constant.** The config holds no contraction value. If any checked layout fails, there is no reference
  and no metric (selftest: oscillating, diverging and |d2| < 1e-4 ladders).

**`l2_extrapolation_verified`** (phase-1 gate; founder item 2). It fails if any of the following holds:
- a checked layout's d1 or d2 is unresolvable: |d| < 1e-4, or its solver-bound interval contains 0. This is the explicit
  |d2| < 1e-4 branch, and it **fails** with the reason;
- r ∉ (0, 1);
- r lies outside the hull of the *other* checked layouts' widened r intervals;
- |two-level with the leave-one-out mean ratio / own L3 − 1| ≥ conv_tol;
- [r_lo, r_hi] ⊄ (0, 1), because the band would then be unbounded.

**`l2_converged`** (conv_tol on the reference error, all layouts).
- Quantity: e_i = |D1_i|·max|ρ(r̂ ± dr) − ρ(r̂)|/L2_inf + (t(k*)L(k*)(1 + ŝ) + t(k*−1)L(k*−1)ŝ)/L2_inf, with ρ(r) = r/(1 − r)
  and dr = the largest leave-one-out deviation |r_j − r̄₋ⱼ| among the checked layouts.
- Why: a layout's extrapolation is as wrong as its contraction is different from r̂, scaled by its first step.
- Where it is evaluated: on the checked layouts in phase 1, and on all 90 after phase 2. It includes the solver bounds
  (T0097 (i)).
- Inference: at the dev dr ≈ 0.03 it fails only for |d1| ≳ 4.5 %. The largest dev |d1| is 2.1 %.

**Unchanged checks:** `all_drc_valid`, `l2_reference_valid`, `l2_layout_reference_valid`, `l2_solver_ok`,
`l2_provenance_consistent`, `all_finite` (now L2_inf too), `sample_diverse` (CV of L2_inf ≥ 0.3, balanced strata).

### 3.3 Metrics (declared list = what the runner emits; checked before any solve)
- **Primary (A2-A5, K1-K3) vs L2_inf.** `spearman_rho_Lloop` and `mape_Lloop` with paired percentile bootstrap CIs
  (2000 resamples, seed 20261008); `top_decile_recall`, read as a point.
- **Sensitivity (T0097), no extra solves.** The same three metrics vs L2(k*) (`*_vs_kstar`) and vs L2(k*−1)
  (`*_vs_kstar_m1`), plus `spearman_L2inf_vs_L2kstar`.
- **Reference-band readings.** The band is one parameter s for all layouts at once, because the ratio is common. Over
  s ∈ [s_lo, s_hi]:
  - `spearman_rho_Lloop_refband_lo/hi` and `mape_Lloop_refband_lo/hi`: the 2.5 % quantile of the per-resample minimum and
    the 97.5 % quantile of the maximum, on a 101-point grid plus ŝ (an inf/sup bootstrap, valid for every s at once);
  - `top_decile_recall_refband_lo/hi`: exact, evaluated at every order-constant point of the band.
  - Reference-robust readings: A2 iff spearman refband_lo ≥ 0.85; K1 iff refband_hi < 0.80; A4 iff mape refband_hi
    ≤ 0.12; K2 iff refband_lo > 0.20; A5/K3 iff both recall ends agree.
  - These readings are conditional on the common-ratio model. Per-layout departures from it are bounded by
    `l2_converged`.
- **Reference model.**
  - Contraction: `l2_contraction_mean`, `_obs_min/max`, `_band_lo/hi`.
  - Leave-one-out error: `l2_extrap_loo_err_max`, plus `_worst_tol_max` (reported, not gated; §0 item 2).
  - Reference error: `l2_ref_pred_err_max`.
  - Raw bias of L2(k*): `l2_kstar_bias_mean` (CI), `_max`, and per family.
  - Band width: `l2_ref_band_rel_lo/hi`.
  - `l2_d1_ratio_max`: how far the sample's first steps reach beyond the verified ones.
- **Secondary (as before).** Within-family Spearman; `cv_L2`; `cv_loop_area`; F4 from L1 only (5/80 vs 20 MHz; L2 at
  5/80 MHz is dropped to fit the wall budget); F3 residual correlations at L1 4 mm and 0.5 mm; L1 mesh bias; cost.
- **F3 rule (pre-registered, unchanged).**
  - A die-count flag is raised if the CI of `resid_spearman_dies` excludes 0.
  - The flag is attributed to L1 discretisation only if the CI of `resid_fine_spearman_dies` includes 0 *and* the CI of
    `l1_mesh_bias_spearman_dies` excludes 0 with the same sign.
  - Otherwise it points at missing physics.

## 5. Power (`research/e002_design/power.json`, T0076 simulation, reviewers verified; rows for n = 60 / 90)
- **The reference error is negligible for power (inference).** The cross-validated reference error is ≤ 0.4 % on dev.

  L2_inf. The family bias offsets β of the T0076 error model shrink to ~0 against L2_inf. That changes MAPE levels, not

## 8. What would show this design wrong
- **Cost.** `cpu_hours_total` > 172 × 1.15, or `l2_wall_hours` > 22 h (the §4 model is wrong). A timeout at 26 h loses
  the run.
- **The reference.** Any of: a gate failure; `l2_extrap_loo_err_max` close to 1 %; `l2_d1_ratio_max` well above 1.5
  (the sample reaches beyond the verified steps); `spearman_L2inf_vs_L2kstar` < 0.99 (the extrapolation reorders the
  reference, so the ranking verdict depends on the reference model). Then read the `*_vs_kstar` metrics alongside.
- **The band is too narrow or too wide.** If the solver bound is realistic, the refband readings are the honest ones. If
  it is loose (§1.1), they are very conservative. A tighter plane-table tolerance at k*+1 is the lever. That is an L2
  change and a new experiment id.
- **Clustering hides a poor model.** `spearman_rho_within_family` far below the pooled ρ. It is reported, not gated.

## 9. E003 (unchanged from T0076)
(T0076 text, kept for reference.)
- **Unit timed:** one `loop_inductance(layout, [20 MHz])` call, Layout → L_loop including discretisation, default mesh.
- **Sample:** sealed seed, `generate_layouts(150)`, 10 per stratum, 3 repeats in a seeded shuffled order (n_evals 450).
- **Warm-up:** 5 calls on a dev-seed layout, declared and excluded. No other exclusion.
- **Threads.** The BLAS/OpenMP variables are set to 1 before numpy is imported. `XLA_FLAGS="--xla_cpu_multi_thread_eigen=false"`.
  - **Why the other token was dropped, not prefixed (fact, this machine, jax 0.11.2):** with
    `XLA_FLAGS="--xla_cpu_multi_thread_eigen=false --intra_op_parallelism_threads=1"`, importing JAX aborts with
    `F… parse_flags_from_env.cc:234] Unknown flag in XLA_FLAGS: --intra_op_parallelism_threads=1`.
    `intra_op_parallelism_threads` is a TensorFlow session option, not an XLA flag. With only the first token, JAX
    imports and computes normally. So "every token carries `--`" is met by keeping only valid flags.
  - `single_thread_enforced` now also fails if any XLA_FLAGS token lacks `--`.
- **Pinning.**
  - Linux: `sched_setaffinity` to one CPU, checked (affinity set of size 1).
  - **macOS cannot pin a process to a core, and cannot choose P- or E-cores.** The scheduler decides. The runner records
    this in `run.tools` (`core_pinning`, `cores`: "4 x Super, 6 x Efficiency" on this Apple M5) and in the check's
    detail.
  - CPU/wall ≤ 1.1 catches extra threads, not E-core placement. An E-core only slows the loop, which is conservative for
    A6 (a ≤ criterion). Running E003 on Linux, where affinity is enforced, gives the cleaner claim.
